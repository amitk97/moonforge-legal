Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short: the game does not collect or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet connection. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Moonforge: Factory Defense ("the game") is an independent game by the developer amitk97. Contact: moonforgefd.support@gmail.com (more on the Support page).

Data we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information, and it requests no Android permissions.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings, campaign progress, MoonBucks balance, saved schematics and any maps you make in the Sector Maker or Test Lab in the game's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device through the game. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Android cloud backup of this data is turned off.

Internet, ads and analytics

The game does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party data collection.

Purchases

If you buy the game or any in-game item through Google Play, the payment is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Contacting us

If you email moonforgefd.support@gmail.com, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and do not share them. If you contact us through GitHub, GitHub's own privacy policy applies to what you post there. Please do not post personal information in a public issue.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts collecting data (for example, through online features), this policy will be updated before that change is released, and the date above will change.
