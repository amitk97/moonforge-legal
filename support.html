Support

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/amitk97/moonforge-legal/issues (a free GitHub account is needed). Issues are public, so please do not include personal information.

Helpful details: your device and Android version (or PC), the game version (shown in Settings), the sector and what you were doing.

Common questions

Do I need an account or internet? No. The game works fully offline.

Where are my saves? On your device only. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Refunds? Purchases through Google Play are refunded through Google Play.
